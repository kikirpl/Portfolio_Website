# Surface brief: portfolio home (single page)

Scope: the whole one-page portfolio. Visitor mode: Experience (the work leads from the first viewport).
Audience/job: recruiters and clients with ~30 seconds deciding whether to contact him. Action: view projects, open a live site, open the CV, email/LinkedIn.
Proof: real previews of shipped sites; experience entries; CV. Constraints: copy verbatim from the importer; no invented claims.
Memorable moment: the camera flying into a glowing screen and the whole interface taking that project's colour.
Unresolved: production domain (canonical/OG URLs); names for two previews without data.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The work is the light. A gallery at night in which his shipped websites are the only light sources; refuses the category default of a split hero with avatar and a grid of equal project cards.

OWN-WORLD: Graphite-black ground (#07080B) with cool moonlight fill; smoked glass, dark brushed-metal bezels, a polished obsidian floor with real reflections and soft shadows. Porcelain-white Archivo, expanded width for display and normal width for text; Martian Mono only for dates, counters and tech tags. Colour comes from each project's sampled accent, which lights the scene and tints the UI while that project is in view. No neon outlines, no gradient text, no eyebrow labels.

STORY: The visitor reads his name and what he does over a ring of his real websites orbiting a faceted core; scrolls into a corridor of screens, each lighting the floor in its own colour; opens one (the camera flies in) to see it large and visit it; passes through lit gates of his experience; ends at contact with email, LinkedIn, GitHub and CV.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Desktop: left ~45%: "Hello World, I'm" leading into the name in expanded Archivo (~5.5rem), rotating roles, the About headline sentence, primary "View projects" and secondary CV action. Right ~55%: 3D ring of the 10 preview screens around a faceted core, tilting toward the pointer. Top bar: RM1 + section links; right edge: position rail. Mobile: ring in the top half, text below, actions in thumb reach.

FORM: Night Gallery, brief-pinned (owner fixed "dark futuristic" and asked for a self-chosen concept), first on the ordered list. seed: none (unavailable)

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
