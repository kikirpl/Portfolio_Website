# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

delegated: Astro 5 static output with one React Three Fiber island (three.js, drei, postprocessing), Lenis for smooth scroll, and a sharp-based content importer. Chosen because every word ships as pre-rendered semantic HTML (SEO, screen readers, 2D fallback) while WebGL is a progressive layer; deploys as static files to Vercel, Netlify or GitHub Pages.

## Users

Inferred from the written brief (the owner asked not to be interviewed): recruiters and prospective clients evaluating Rizky Maulana Ibrohim, often with about 30 seconds, on a laptop or a phone. Their job is to decide whether he can build what they need: who he is, what he has shipped, his recent experience, and how to reach him.

## Product Purpose

Personal portfolio of Rizky Maulana Ibrohim, full-stack developer. Success: within seconds a visitor knows who he is and what he does, has seen real websites he built, and can open the CV or contact him.

## Positioning

The proof is the work itself: previews of real company websites he shipped (gold and Web3, agribusiness, food, construction, education) are the centrepiece, and the site is itself a demonstration of the craft he sells.

## Operating Context

Visitors arrive from a CV, LinkedIn, GitHub or a shared link, on desktop and mobile, usually skimming before deciding to read.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Every piece of copy comes verbatim from the source repo `kikirpl/portfolio_website` through `npm run import`; no invented claims, dates, names or links. Typos and odd data are reported in IMPORT_REPORT.md, not silently fixed.
- Content on hand: name, greeting, roles, two About paragraphs, a skills list, 8 named projects plus 2 previews without names, 6 experience entries, CV PDF, email, LinkedIn, GitHub.
- Site language is English, because all site text in the repo is English.
- Must work without WebGL (a 2D version), honour reduced motion, and support mouse, touch and keyboard.
- Performance target: 60 fps on a mid-range laptop, at least 30 fps on a mid-range phone.

## Brand Commitments

- Dark mode by default; "futuristik gelap" (dark futuristic) style, pinned by the owner.
- Brand mark "RM1" from the previous site.
- The owner likes poggufanz.xyz as a reference; it could not be viewed from this environment (blocked by network policy).

## Evidence on Hand

- `public/content/profile.json`, `projects.json`, `experience.json`, preview images and the CV, all generated by the importer.
- No testimonials, metrics, client logos or awards exist; none may be fabricated.

## Product Principles

1. The work leads: previews are the visual centre, at a scale where they read as real websites.
2. Clarity before spectacle: name, role and a path to the projects are readable at once, before the 3D finishes loading.
3. Every word is his: the interface only adds labels and navigation.
4. Spectacle degrades gracefully: low-end, no-WebGL and reduced-motion visitors get the same content, calmly.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Semantic HTML for all content, meaningful alt text, visible keyboard focus, prefers-reduced-motion support, WCAG AA contrast.
